# 30-Day GTM Planner

One wedge, one format, one goal. Everything else is deferred.

## Inputs
Product one-liner, target wedge (agencies, creators, or brands), team size and weekly hours, budget, launch date, existing audience (if any).

## Workflow
1. **Pick the goal** (choose one): N paying customers, N design partners, N waitlist signups, or $ MRR. State the number.
2. **ICP + positioning** using `assets/icp-and-positioning.md`. Include the "why now" and the alternative the customer uses today.
3. **Offer + pricing** using `assets/pricing-worksheet.md`. Include a founding-customer offer.
4. **Choose at most 3 channels** and justify each with reach, cost, and time-to-signal.
5. **Weekly plan:** W1 Foundations and demo, W2 Discovery and waitlist, W3 Private beta and proof, W4 Public launch and paid test. Expand each into daily actions with owner, time estimate, and output.
6. **Outreach kit:** `assets/outreach-sequences.md` (cold DM, email, follow-ups).
7. **Metrics and kill/continue rules:** leading indicators per week, and what result on day 30 means "double down", "adjust", or "stop".

## Output
`30-day-gtm.md` with a table of days, plus a one-page summary at the top (goal, ICP, offer, channels, metrics).

## Guardrails
- Budget and hours must fit the team's real capacity; show the arithmetic.
- No plan without at least 10 direct customer conversations.
- Mark assumptions and how each will be tested by day 30.
