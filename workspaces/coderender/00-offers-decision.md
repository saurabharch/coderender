## ICP

| Field | Answer |
|-------|--------|
| Who | Owner or manager of a 2–10 person local business in one of 10 verticals: salon owners, gym/fitness centres, bakers & cake shops, doctors & health clinics, restaurant & bars, pest control, car garages & mechanics, tours & travels, yoga & wellness, handyman services |
| Trigger event | Slow weeks, new competitor opened nearby, bad/zero Google reviews, missed calls and WhatsApp enquiries going unanswered, no website or a dead one |
| Current alternative | DIY Google profile + Instagram posting when they remember, a cousin-built website, paying a freelancer per-post, newspaper/flyer spend |
| Cost of the problem | Owner spends 5–8 hrs/week on follow-ups and posting; 3–5 missed enquiries/week at ~₹1,500–₹5,000 average ticket depending on vertical |
| Where they hang out | Google search for their own category, WhatsApp business groups, local Instagram, Justdial/IndiaMART, neighbourhood associations |
| Disqualifiers | No named approver available weekly · wants ranking/revenue guarantees · budget under ₹3,000 total · asks us to run personal social accounts or post fake reviews |

Positioning: for local owners who lose enquiries to competitors that look better on Google, coderender is a fixed-scope growth service that tunes their Google presence, follow-up and landing page in weeks. Unlike per-post freelancers, we ship a dated, capped-scope pack and then maintain it on a quota.

### Rung 1 — GBP Audit + Rank Report (₹2,999 DRAFT, 3 days)

- Inputs needed: Google Business Profile access (manager role), list of 3 competitors, 5 business photos from the owner.
- Deliverables: 12-point profile audit, category/keyword check for their vertical, photo + review-request gap list, 1-page fix priority report, 15-min walkthrough call.
- Exclusions: no edits made to the profile itself · no review responses written · no website work · no ad spend or campaign setup.
- Change-request rule: 1 revision round on the report within 3 days. Anything else (extra locations, extra competitors, implementation) is a written change request with price and date before work starts.

## Capacity model (70% utilization)

Available: 160 hrs/mo (one delivery person). Billable cap at 70% = 112 hrs/mo.

| Offer | Hours per delivery | Deliveries/mo | Hours used |
|-------|--------------------|---------------|------------|
| GBP Audit + Rank Report | 3 | 8 | 24 |
| Growth Pack (avg across tiers) | 24 | 2 | 48 |
| Retainer (avg across tiers) | 16 | 2 | 32 |
| Buffer (sales, fixes, admin) | — | — | 8 |
| **Total** | | | **112 = cap** |

Rule: 112 used = 112 available. A third concurrent Growth Pack, a third retainer, or audits beyond 8/mo means raise prices, cut scope, or add a delivery person — in that order. Retainers get scheduling priority; diagnostics fill gaps.
