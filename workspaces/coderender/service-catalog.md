## 1. Google Business Profile (GMB) setup & tune-up

- Outcome promised: a complete, accurate profile with correct categories, hours, services, photos and Q&A, plus a review-request flow the owner's staff can run in under 5 minutes a day.
- Inputs needed: GBP manager access, 3 competitors, 10+ business photos, service + price list, opening hours.
- Deliverables: full profile tune-up, 4 scheduled Google posts, review-request QR card + WhatsApp template, 20 review-reply templates in the owner's voice, before/after snapshot.
- Timeline: audit in 3 days; Sprint tune-up go-live in 2 weeks.
- Client responsibilities: grant access within 2 business days, supply photos and price list, approve copy within 2 business days per round.
- Excluded: fake or incentivised reviews, more than 1 location, ongoing posting (retainer).

## 2. Website development (vertical landing page / site)

- Outcome promised: one fast, mobile-first landing page for the business's money service (booking, quote, or visit), with a working lead form and call/WhatsApp buttons — live on the client's domain within 2 weeks.
- Inputs needed: logo and brand colours (or permission to pick), service list + prices, 8+ photos, testimonials if available, domain access.
- Deliverables: copy written for one vertical + one locality, page design + build, lead form wired to enquiry inbox, call/WhatsApp sticky bar, basic on-page SEO (title, meta, schema), 2-week bug hypercare.
- Timeline: 2 weeks from content receipt; clock pauses if content is late.
- Client responsibilities: one named approver, content and feedback within 2 business days, domain/DNS access.
- Excluded: multi-page sites beyond the agreed scope (quoted separately), logo redesign, copy in more than 1 language, custom web apps or booking-engine builds.

## 3. Local SEO

- Outcome promised: consistent business listings across 15+ directories, location pages that read correctly for customers and search engines, and a monthly snapshot showing profile views, calls, and direction requests.
- Inputs needed: business registration details, service areas with pincodes, existing logins for directories, photo bank.
- Deliverables: citation cleanup + 15 listings, on-page local SEO for 1 site, review-velocity plan, monthly visibility report (views/calls/directions — reported numbers, never promised improvements).
- Timeline: 4-week project, then optional monthly from ₹11,999 DRAFT.
- Client responsibilities: confirm listing details within 3 business days, never share review-gating logins, route customer complaints to the owner not us.
- Excluded: ranking or traffic guarantees, link-buying schemes, more than 1 location per pack.

## 4. SEO marketing (content)

- Outcome promised: 4 ready-to-publish posts per month (Google posts + one blog/ locality article) written for one vertical, delivered as a scheduled calendar the owner can approve in one sitting.
- Inputs needed: top 5 services to push, 3 locality keywords, seasonal offers calendar, approval on tone from 2 sample posts.
- Deliverables: monthly content calendar, 4 written + formatted posts, 1 locality article (600–800 words), publishing to GBP + site included on Standard retainer and above.
- Timeline: first calendar within 10 days, then monthly.
- Client responsibilities: one approval sitting per month, flag offers/stock-outs before the 25th.
- Excluded: video shoots, posting to the owner's personal accounts, performance guarantees on any single post.

## 5. Lead generation (ads + funnels)

- Outcome promised: one tracked campaign (Google or Meta) sending clicks to the client's landing page with call/WhatsApp tracking, plus a weekly spend-and-lead sheet the owner can read in 2 minutes.
- Inputs needed: monthly ad budget paid directly by client (min ₹10,000), offer or hook to advertise, decision on Google vs Meta from the audit, landing page live.
- Deliverables: campaign setup, 2 starting creatives, conversion tracking to the lead form, weekly report, 4 creative refreshes/mo on Plus retainer.
- Timeline: setup in 2 weeks; optimisation is monthly.
- Client responsibilities: fund ad account before launch, answer leads fast (speed is the client's lever), approve creatives within 2 business days.
- Excluded: the ad spend itself, cost-per-lead or ROAS guarantees, creative beyond the agreed quota, landing-page rebuilds (quoted separately).

## 6. Chat automation (WhatsApp AI agent / flows)

- Outcome promised: every new enquiry and missed call gets an instant, polite WhatsApp reply and a follow-up sequence, so no lead waits overnight — with a shared inbox the staff already knows how to use.
- Inputs needed: WhatsApp Business number + API access, list of top 10 customer questions with the owner's real answers, service/price/menu data, staff member for 1 training call.
- Deliverables: 2 flows (new enquiry + missed-call recovery), FAQ auto-replies for top 10 questions, human-handoff rule, 1 staff training call, 2-week hypercare.
- Timeline: 1–2 weeks standalone; included in week 2–3 of the Growth Pack.
- Client responsibilities: keep the WhatsApp number funded and staffed for handoffs, confirm answers are accurate (we automate the owner's words, not invent policies).
- Excluded: free-form AI that answers without the approved FAQ (guardrails stay on), spam broadcasts to non-opted-in lists, managing the inbox day-to-day.

## 7. Featured tool — GBP Booster, WhatsApp AI Agent

- Outcome promised: the profile, the reviews engine, and the WhatsApp follow-up working as one system — a customer who finds the business on Google can read fresh posts, see answered reviews, tap to chat, and get an instant helpful reply, all delivered as one dated build.
- Inputs needed: everything from lines 1 + 6 combined, plus one 45-min discovery call on the business's busiest enquiry types.
- Deliverables: GBP tune-up + 4 posts, review-request QR + 20 reply templates, 2 WhatsApp flows + FAQ replies, 1 landing page with chat handoff, monthly care option (posts + replies + flow tweaks + snapshot report).
- Timeline: 3 weeks setup; care plan monthly at ₹9,999 DRAFT.
- Client responsibilities: named approver, access within 2 business days, footage/photos supplied, feedback within 2 business days.
- Excluded: ad spend, extra locations, fake reviews, inbox staffing.

## Change-request rule (applies to every line)

2 revision rounds per asset (1 for diagnostics). Anything beyond the deliverables above — extra locations, pages, flows, languages, or rounds — is a written change request stating the added price and the revised date, approved before work starts. Over-quota retainer work: ₹1,500/hr blocks, minimum 2 hrs, agreed in writing first.

Review monthly: demand per line, delivery hours vs plan, margin vs 50% target, client satisfaction. Retire lines below target margin twice running; raise prices when a line is waitlisted.
