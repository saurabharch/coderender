# Customer Discovery Guide (30 min)

Goal: learn how they do this today, not to pitch. Record with permission.

## Opening (3 min)
Explain purpose; no right answers; ask permission to take notes.

## Context (5 min)
1. Tell me about your role and the last creative project you shipped.
2. How many brands/clients do you produce for?

## Current workflow (10 min)
3. Walk me through the last time you made a video/creative from brief to approval.
4. Where did it slow down or go wrong?
5. How do you keep the brand consistent across outputs and people?
6. Who approves, and how many rounds does it take?

## Tools and spend (5 min)
7. Which tools do you use now? What do you pay? What do you dislike?
8. Have you tried AI tools? What happened?

## Value (5 min)
9. If this were solved, what would it be worth per month?
10. What would stop you from trying a new tool?

## Close (2 min)
Ask for a follow-up, referral, and permission to share anonymized quotes.
