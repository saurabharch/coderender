# Discovery Call Script (30-45 min): {{client}}

Goal: understand the problem, its cost, and whether we can fix it profitably. Do not pitch until section 5.

## 1. Open (3 min)
Thanks for the time. I will ask about how things work today, then say honestly whether we are a fit. OK to take notes?

## 2. Situation (7 min)
- Walk me through how {{process}} works today, step by step.
- Who is involved? Which tools/systems?
- How often, and how many per week?

## 3. Pain and cost (10 min)
- What goes wrong? Last example?
- Time per week spent? Errors, delays, lost deals?
- What has been tried? Why did it not stick?
- If unchanged for 6 months, what happens?

## 4. Decision and constraints (7 min)
- Who decides and who must approve? Timeline?
- Budget range or comparable spend?
- Systems access, security or data restrictions? Where must data live?
- What does success look like in numbers?

## 5. Position the next step (8 min)
Summarize back in their words. Propose the entry offer (audit/sprint) or a tailored proposal. Agree on date for proposal and who will review it.

## 6. Close (2 min)
Confirm next steps and send written recap within 24h.
